# ADR-001: License and re-use policy
Status: Accepted
Date: 2026-10-09
Owner: ORCH
Related tickets: SPK-10, REL-03

## Context
Veskra is an independent open-source implementation (MASTER_PLAN §0). Krita is GPL; copying it would
force GPL obligations on the whole product. Rust ecosystem norms favour MIT OR Apache-2.0.

## Decision
All Veskra source is dual-licensed **MIT OR Apache-2.0** (`LICENSE-MIT`, `LICENSE-APACHE`).
No Krita, GPL, proprietary Adobe code, assets or reverse-engineered secrets may be copied.
Third-party dependencies must be permissively licensed (MIT, Apache-2.0, BSD, Zlib, ISC, Unicode,
MPL-2.0 only after review); `deny.toml` enforces the allow-list in CI. All bundled data (ICC
profiles, fixtures, brushes) must be generated by Veskra or carry recorded provenance in
`tests/fixtures/LICENSES.md`.

## Alternatives considered
1. GPL-3.0 to allow Krita reuse — rejected: contradicts the independent-implementation plan,
   complicates app-store distribution and permissive embedding of the engine.
2. Apache-2.0 only — viable; dual license matches Rust ecosystem and maximizes reuse.

## Acceptance experiment
`cargo deny check licenses` passes in CI with the allow-list; every fixture has a provenance entry.

## Consequences
Implementations of algorithms (curve fitting, ICC math, PSD layout) are written from public
papers/specifications. ICC profiles are generated in-tree (ADR-006), avoiding redistribution of
third-party profile bytes.

## Rollback / fallback
Any GPL reuse proposal requires a new ADR with legal review (DECISIONS_AND_RISKS Q8).
